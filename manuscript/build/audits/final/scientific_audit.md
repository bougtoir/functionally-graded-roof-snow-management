Suitable as a reduced-order hypothesis and design-space study if the limitations remain prominent; not suitable as structural or public-safety validation.

## Checks

- Primary outcomes match the frozen definitions.
- Uniform optimization precedes heterogeneous comparison.
- Geometry-only, surface-only, and joint ablations are retained.
- Mass, age moment, and snow volume are conserved through transport.
- Density is limited to depth diagnostics and depth-to-mass conversion.
- Wind redistribution, fracture, impact, structure, and exposure are explicitly outside scope.
