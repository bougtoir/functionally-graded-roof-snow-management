# Formatting, language, and consistency audit

- Manuscript is an editable, single-column DOCX with continuous line numbering requested in WordprocessingML.
- Figures are separate PNG, 1000 dpi TIFF, SVG, PDF, and EPS files.
- Tables in the manuscript are editable; the full phase table is CSV.
- Citations are numbered in first-appearance order.
- Figure and table labels are English throughout.
- “Modeled roof snow mass” is used instead of “structural load” for computed outcomes.
- Claims are conditional and distinguish synthetic, JMA, and empirical validation evidence.
