# CONSTRAINED_OPTIMIZATION_AUDIT (R1)

Machine-readable: `results/r1/optimization/*` (per-run evaluated designs, generation history, metadata), `results/r1/pareto/run_diagnostics.csv`, `front_summary.csv`, `uniform_gap_coverage.csv`. Scripts: `scripts/r1_optimize.py`, `scripts/r1_analyze.py`.

## Problem definition (revised)
Minimise (L_max, S_event,max), worst case over three synthetic winters, dt = 1 h (pre-specified convergence rule). Every candidate is generated by the feasible decoder in `src/graded_roof/optimization_v2.py`: piecewise-constant segments of ≥ 3 cells, ≤ 6 transitions, integer slopes 2–45°, |Δslope| ≤ 12° between adjacent segments, and one of three discrete surface classes per segment. Constructability is therefore satisfied by construction for 100 % of evaluated candidates (`feasible_fraction` = 1.0 in all 15 runs); no post hoc mapping is used.

## Comparison sets and budgets
| Set | Search | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Uniform (constructable) | exhaustive: 44 integer slopes × 3 classes | 132 designs |
| Geometry-only | NSGA-II, slopes graded, single class | 48 × 45 = 2,160 evaluations per seed, 5 seeds |
| Surface-only | NSGA-II, classes graded, single slope | same |
| Joint | NSGA-II, slopes and classes graded | same |
| Uniform continuous (sensitivity) | 30 × 30 × 7 grid, continuous friction/adhesion | 6,300 designs |

Unique simulated designs per run, runtime and seeds are in `run_diagnostics.csv` (1,012–1,874 unique designs; 58–138 s per run on 2 cores).

## Diagnostics reported
Unique objective pairs and nondominated designs including ties are reported separately (`front_summary.csv`), because many designs map to the same objective pair (e.g. the joint set has 86 unique nondominated pairs represented by 1,070 designs).

## Uniform-gap coverage (descriptive, defined after inspecting the fronts)
The exhaustive constructable uniform front has no design with L_max between the shedding regime (91.9 kg m⁻¹) and full retention (6,417 kg m⁻¹). `uniform_gap_coverage.csv` counts nondominated graded pairs inside this interval for every mode and every seed. This is a post hoc descriptive summary and is labelled as such in the manuscript; the pre-specified comparison metrics are hypervolume, additive epsilon and matched-objective improvement (`PARETO_RELIABILITY_AUDIT.md`).

## Limitations
NSGA-II is heuristic; graded fronts are approximations whose quality is assessed by seed variability and budget sensitivity only. The uniform reference is exact for the constructable set but not for continuous properties (sensitivity grid supplied).
