# PHYSICAL_EVIDENCE_GAP (R1)

1. **No roof-scale observations of release timing or mass** for graded roofs exist in the project; JMA station data describe ground snow, not roof snow.
2. **Friction and adhesion values** for the three surface classes are assumptions bounded by qualitative literature; measured μs, μk, c for specific roofing products under wet/dry snow were not available as data.
3. **Ageing and near-melt friction laws** are assumed functional forms.
4. **Slab mechanics**: rigid-block runout with inelastic entrainment ignores slab fracture, cornice formation, eave overhang, snow guards and creep/glide.
5. **Hydrology**: meltwater and rain leave the roof instantly; no liquid retention, refreezing, ice dams or drainage triggering.
6. **Wind redistribution and drift** are excluded (see CFD literature: Tominaga 2018; Zhou et al. 2016, 2023).
7. **Structural load** is not computed; retained mass per strip is not a code design load.

Required to close the gap: (a) product-specific inclined-plane friction/adhesion tests across temperature and liquid-water content; (b) instrumented roof panels (load cells + time-lapse) such as the Tominaga et al. 2025 setup, ideally with graded test panels; (c) eave-catchment measurement of release mass and timing.
