# Phase 04 handoff: knee and matched trade-offs

Status: PASS

## Findings

- The joint knee changes from `3210.636/39.9995` to
  `2941.908/43.3328 kg m-1` under common combined normalization.
- The original knee percentage is not a comparable headline effect.
- Six joint pairs occupy objective regions unavailable to the uniform set under
  equal-or-tighter simultaneous caps.
- Prespecified `S_max` limits of 20, 80, and 200 kg m-1 were evaluated without
  adding post hoc thresholds.
